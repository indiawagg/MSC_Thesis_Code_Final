Article Title:
The Daily — Tourism Human Resource Module
Article Content:
Released: 2024 07 26
The Tourism Human Resource Module by occupation is now available upon request. This builds on the previous estimates by characteristics released on June 7, 2024, by adding the occupation details. It depicts annual estimates of jobs, hours worked, and wages and salaries in tourism industries consistent with the System of National Accounts concepts, by characteristics of the employees holding those jobs.
The Economic accounts statistics portal, which can be accessed through the Subjects module of our website, features an up to date portrait of the national and provincial economies and their structure.
Latest Developments in the Canadian Economic Accounts (Catalogue number13 605 X) is available.
User Guide: Canadian System of Macroeconomic Accounts (Catalogue number13 606 G) is also available.
Methodological Guide: Canadian System of Macroeconomic Accounts (Catalogue number13 607 X) is also available.
