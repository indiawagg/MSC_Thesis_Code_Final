Article Title:
The Daily — Education Indicators in Canada
Article Content:
Released: 2024 10 22
New data from the Pan Canadian Education Indicators Program are now available, including updated tables about demographic characteristics of the school age population; education finance; high school graduation rates; teachers, the learning environment and the organization of schools; research and development; and transitions to postsecondary education and the labour market.
The October 2024 issue of the publication Education Indicators in Canada: Report of the Pan Canadian Education Indicators Program, October 2024 (Catalogue number81 582 X) is now available.
