Article Title:
The Daily — Survey of Innovation and Business Strategy: Data tables, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 09 27
Data on sales, expenditures and personnel of Canadian enterprises in 2022 are now available from the Survey of Innovation and Business Strategy.
Results are available by industry and broken down by enterprise size and region.
