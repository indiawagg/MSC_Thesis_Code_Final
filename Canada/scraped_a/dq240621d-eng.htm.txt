Article Title:
The Daily — Annual civil aviation statistics: First look, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 06 21
Canadian air carriers transported 89.9 million passengers in 2023, an increase of 32.3% from the 67.9 million passengers carried in 2022 and accounting for 95.5% of the 94.1 million passengers carried in 2019, before the COVID 19 pandemic.
These preliminary estimates provide a first look at key variables, including passengers, total traffic, revenues and expenses. The first look was introduced at the onset of the pandemic, to gauge impacts on commercial aviation in a timely manner.
Recovery of the airline industry began in 2021 and accelerated into 2022 with the lifting of all COVID 19 travel restrictions. It continued at a slower pace in 2023, as air traffic approached pre pandemic levels but was tempered by wildfires during the busy summer travel season (see Monthly civil aviation statistics, July 2023). 
Domestic traffic reached 44.4 million passengers in 2023, up 23.5% from 2022 and accounting for 91.0% of the 2019 pre pandemic level. International traffic, at 45.4 million passengers in 2023, increased 42.1% from 2022 and was slightly above (+0.2%) the 2019 pre pandemic level.
Total traffic reached 228.5 billion passenger kilometres in 2023, 35.5% higher than in 2022 and 2.0% below the 2019 output. Each passenger travelled an average of 2,542 kilometres in 2023, 2.6% above the 2019 level (2,478 kilometres). At 3.1 million hours, total hours flown in 2023 were 5.8% below the 2019 level.
Operating revenue earned by Level I to III air carriers in 2023 totalled $35.7 billion, 21.1% higher than in 2019. Passenger revenue was 19.0% higher in 2023 than the 2019 level.
Operating expenses ($32.8 billion) also rose in 2023 and were 19.8% higher than in 2019. With increased traffic in 2023, fuel consumption was 21.5% higher than in 2022 and 1.6% below the 2019 level. Due to lower aviation fuel prices in 2023 (see the Industrial Product Price Index), fuel costs (+12.5%) grew at a slower rate than in 2022.
In 2023, employment in the airline industry increased 3.8% from 2019, while wages and salaries were 37.5% higher than in 2019, contributing to higher operating expenses.
