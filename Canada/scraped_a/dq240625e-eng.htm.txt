Article Title:
The Daily — Infographic: Gender diversity of Canadian postsecondary students, 2021/2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 06 25
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing an infographic entitled "Gender diversity of Canadian postsecondary students, 2021/2022." Using data from the Postsecondary Student Information System and the Census of Population, 2021, this infographic provides information on enrolment in Canadian public postsecondary institutions for transgender and non binary people.
The infographic, "Gender diversity of Canadian postsecondary students, 2021/2022," part of the Statistics Canada – Infographics series (Catalogue number11 627 M), is now available.
