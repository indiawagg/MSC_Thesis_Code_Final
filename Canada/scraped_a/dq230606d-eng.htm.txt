Article Title:
The Daily — Results of a feasibility study — Students in private postsecondary education, 2020
Article Content:
Released: 2023 06 06
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing an infographic entitled "Students in private postsecondary education, 2020: Results of a feasibility study," along with an associated methodological document entitled "Students in private postsecondary education, 2020: A feasibility study." 
Although Statistics Canada has long published education indicators for students attending public postsecondary institutions in Canada, data on private postsecondary education have not been explored at the same depth. This infographic and methodological document address this data gap using a new data source, the T2202 Tuition and Enrolment Certificate, included in the Education and Labour Market Longitudinal Platform. 
The infographic Students in private postsecondary education, 2020: Results of a feasibility study, part of the Statistics Canada – Infographics series (Catalogue number11 627 M), is now available.
The associated methodological document Students in private postsecondary education, 2020: A feasibility study, part of the Technical Reference Guides for the Education and Labour Market Longitudinal Platform series (37 20 0001), is now available.
