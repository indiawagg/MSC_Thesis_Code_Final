Article Title:
The Daily — Study: 2022 flash estimates of selected services industries
Article Content:
Released: 2023 08 15
Today, Statistics Canada is publishing a new article entitled "Strong revenue growth for service industries in 2022." According to this study, the resilience of Canadian businesses helped to mitigate the effects of changing monetary and economic conditions to maintain solid revenue growth in service industries in 2022. Tourism related service industries experienced the strongest rate of growth. 
Labour market challenges sustained robust revenue growth in employment services, while rising interest rates slowed growth in architectural services. In general, for most service industries, revenue growth in 2022 was above trends seen before the COVID 19 pandemic. 
The study "Strong revenue growth for service industries in 2022," which is part of Analysis in Brief (Catalogue number11 621 M), is now available.
