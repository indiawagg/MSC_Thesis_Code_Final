Article Title:
The Daily — Latin American Immigration to Canada: An overview
Article Content:
Released: 2024 10 29
In honour of Latin American Heritage Month, Statistics Canada is happy to release the infographic "Latin American Immigration to Canada." This infographic provides data trends on the size of the Latin American population from 1996 to 2021 for immigrants, non immigrants and non permanent residents, as well as admission categories and the composition of the Latin American immigrant population in Canada, by the top 10 countries of birth.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
