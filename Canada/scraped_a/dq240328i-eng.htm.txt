Article Title:
The Daily — Quality of life indicators for the "Selected indicators of quality of life in Canada" dashboard
Article Content:
Released: 2024 03 28
The data visualization product Selected indicators of quality of life in Canada has been updated with four additional quality of life indicators. These indicators, which are part of the Quality of Life Framework for Canada, include: difficulty meeting financial needs, confidence in institutions, satisfaction with time use, and satisfaction with local environment.
