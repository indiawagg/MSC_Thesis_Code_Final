Article Title:
The Daily — Activities of multinational enterprises in Canada and abroad, update of tables, 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2024 05 21
Data tables for the activities of multinational enterprises in Canada have been updated to include gross domestic product for the reference year 2021.
More detailed information can be found in the product "Activities of Multinational Enterprises: Interactive Tool."
