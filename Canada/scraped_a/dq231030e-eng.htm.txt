Article Title:
The Daily — Monthly estimates of business openings and closures, July 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2023 10 30
Monthly estimates of business openings and closures are now available for July 2023.
This update also includes a new table on monthly business openings and closures by employment size, as a result of a collaboration between Statistics Canada and the Business Data Lab.
In addition to the data tables published this morning, a short article is also available in StatsCAN Plus.
