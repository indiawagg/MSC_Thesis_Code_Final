Article Title:
The Daily — Farm Financial Survey, 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2023 02 24
Information from the Farm Financial Survey for 2021 is now available. The Farm Financial Survey provides data on average farm assets, liabilities, capital investments, capital sales, revenues and expenses.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
