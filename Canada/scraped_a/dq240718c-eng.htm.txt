Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian potato production, June 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 07 18
Data on the seeded area of potatoes are now available for 2024.
Potato farmers planted 393,592 acres (159 281 hectares) of potatoes in 2024, down 0.9% compared with 2023.
Prince Edward Island continued to report the largest seeded area in 2024, at 85,300 acres, followed closely by Manitoba (78,600 acres) and Alberta (75,500 acres).
