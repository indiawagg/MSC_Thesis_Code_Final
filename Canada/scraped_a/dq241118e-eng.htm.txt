Article Title:
The Daily — Urban public transit, September 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 18
In September, Canada's urban transit systems logged nearly 141 million passenger trips, the highest monthly total since the beginning of the COVID 19 pandemic. 
Ridership in September 2024 reached 85.9% of the pre pandemic level from the same month in 2019.
Total passenger trips and operating revenue for selected urban transit agencies are available in table 23 10 0307 01.
In September 2024, there were 140.9 million total passenger trips taken on Canada's urban transit systems, up 4.4% (+6.0 million trips) from September 2023. In fact, ridership in September 2024 was the highest monthly total since March 2020, recovering 85.9% of the pre pandemic level from September 2019. Despite this milestone, there were about 23.1 million fewer rides in September 2024 compared with the same month of 2019.
In the two years prior to the pandemic (2018 and 2019), public transit ridership increased by an average of 11.6% from August to September, as school resumed and workers returned from summer vacations. The increase of 13.4% (+16.7 million rides) from August to September 2024 may reflect continued employment growth, with many employers now requiring more on site work. 
In September 2024, transit agency operating revenues (excluding subsidies) reached $344.8 million, an increase of 6.7% (+$21.5 million) from September 2023. While this total was the highest level recorded since March 2020, it was $24.2 million below the pre pandemic level in September 2019.
