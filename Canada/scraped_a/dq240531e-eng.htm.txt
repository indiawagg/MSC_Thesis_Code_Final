Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian Agricultural Partnership Survey, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 05 31
Data from the 2023 Canadian Agricultural Partnership Survey are now available upon request.
These data include information on the characteristics of agricultural operations who have participated in the Canadian Agricultural Partnership from 2018 to 2023 and the impacts on their businesses and the overall agricultural sector.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
