Article Title:
The Daily — Business Counts in Rural and Small Town Canada: Interactive Dashboard
Article Content:
Released: 2024 09 26
The Business Counts in Rural and Small Town Canada: Interactive Dashboard has been updated with data for the June 2024 reference period. 
Data presented in this interactive dashboard are based on counts of active businesses identified in Statistics Canada's Business Register, primarily compiled from Canada Revenue Agency tax records.
The product "Business Counts in Rural and Small Town Canada: Interactive Dashboard," which is part of the series Statistics Canada – Data Visualization Products (Catalogue number71 607 X), is available on the Rural Canada Statistics portal.
