Article Title:
The Daily — Secondary distributors of refined petroleum products, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 18
Secondary distributors of refined petroleum products sold 23.9 billion litres in Canada in 2023, down 1.5% from 2022. The refined petroleum products sold included motor gasoline (53.1%), diesel fuel oil (41.2%), light fuel oil (3.6%) and heavy fuel oil (2.1%). 
Secondary distributors sold 12.7 billion litres of motor gasoline to end users in 2023, an increase of 1.3% compared with the previous year. The bulk of these sales (90.8%) was made to gasoline stations.
Approximately two thirds (66.2%) of the sales were in Ontario and Quebec, while the rest of Canada made up the remaining one third.
In 2023, secondary distributors sold 9.8 billion litres of diesel fuel oil to end users, down by 3.4% compared with the previous year. The key customer types included gasoline stations (37.2%), agriculture (16.1%), other commercial and institutional customers (13.4%), road transport and support activities (10.6%) and construction (8.5%).
Sales in Ontario and Quebec accounted for just under half (49.6%) of diesel fuel oil sales to end users in 2023.
Secondary distributors sold 870.8 million litres of light fuel oil to end users in 2023, down 14.0% from 2022. In 2023, 68.0% of the light fuel oil sold was to residential customers. 
Customers in the Atlantic provinces (37.6%), Quebec (35.0%) and Ontario (13.6%) purchased most of the light fuel oil.
