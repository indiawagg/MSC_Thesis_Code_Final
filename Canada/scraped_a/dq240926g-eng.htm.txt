Article Title:
The Daily — Business Conditions in Rural and Small Town Canada: Interactive Dashboard
Article Content:
Released: 2024 09 26
Business Conditions in Rural and Small Town Canada: Interactive Dashboard has been updated with data for the first to third quarters of 2024. 
This dashboard presents data based on the quarterly results from the Canadian Survey on Business Conditions, including expectations, obstacles, supply chain challenges, debt and future outlook of businesses in rural and small town areas.
The product "Business Conditions in Rural and Small Town Canada: Interactive Dashboard," which is part of the series Statistics Canada – Data Visualization Products (Catalogue number71 607 X), is available on the Rural Canada Statistics portal.
