Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian Defence, Aerospace, Marine and Cybersecurity Industries Survey, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 02 21
Selected data tables from the 2022 Canadian Defence, Aerospace, Marine and Cybersecurity Industries Survey are now available upon request.
The tables provide data at the national level on goods and services, sales, exports and innovation among firms engaged in the manufacture of products and the delivery of services related to Canada's defence, aerospace, marine and cybersecurity industries. 
For more information about Canada's defence, aerospace, marine and cybersecurity industries, contact Philippe Richer (613 852 3018; philippe.richer@ised isde.gc.ca), Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada.
