Article Title:
The Daily — Study: Pathways from education to the labour market among Canadian youth
Article Content:
Released: 2023 07 28
Roughly 350,000 young Canadians graduate high school each year. How many of them go on to postsecondary studies? How many of them go directly to the labour market? How long does it take to complete their postsecondary studies? How well do they do once they join the labour market? A new fact sheet entitled "From high school, into postsecondary education and on to the labour market" brings together the latest available data to provide insight into the pathways of young Canadians through education and into the labour market. 
The fact sheet "From high school, into postsecondary education and on to the labour market" (Catalogue number81 595 M) is now available.
