Article Title:
The Daily — New data tables from the Canadian Housing Survey, 2018 and 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2023 03 31
New data tables which inform the Housing needs indicator in Canada's Quality of Life Framework are now available for the years 2018 and 2021 from the Canadian Housing Survey. The tables present statistics on acceptable housing and core housing need by province, by housing tenure and by selected sociodemographic characteristics, such as age group, immigrant status and household income.
