Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian postsecondary students and graduates, by racialized group, 2014 to 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2024 06 26
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing data on students enrolled in and graduated from Canadian colleges and universities (cohorts of 2014 to 2021) by racialized group. This release includes information on the number of Canadian enrolled students and graduates by racialized group, educational qualification, field of study, age group, and gender. Data are available at the national, provincial and territorial levels.
The data used for the calculation of these counts are derived from the Postsecondary Student Information System and the Census of Population.
The data visualization tool titled "Racialized postsecondary Canadian students", which is part of the publication Statistics Canada – Data Visualization Products (Catalogue number71 607 X), is now available.
