Article Title:
The Daily — Industrial capacity utilization rates, second quarter 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 09 13
Canadian industries operated at 79.1% of their production capacity in the second quarter, up slightly from 78.6% in the first quarter of 2024, which was revised up slightly by 0.1 percentage point. 
Capacity utilization in the mining, quarrying, and oil and gas extraction sector rose 1.6 percentage points to 77.2% in the second quarter. This increase coincided with higher activity in both oil sands extraction after production resumed in a number of facilities following maintenance and turnarounds late in the first quarter and support activities for mining, and oil and gas extraction.
In contrast, the capacity utilization rate in the construction sector edged down 0.2 percentage points to 82.4% in the second quarter, following a downturn in residential building construction.
The capacity utilization rate in the manufacturing sector rose 0.5 percentage points to 77.6% in the second quarter. This increase was driven by computer and electronic product manufacturing (+5.1 percentage points) and wood product manufacturing (+2.9 percentage points).
