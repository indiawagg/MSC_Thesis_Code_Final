Article Title:
The Daily — Retail Commodity Survey, August 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 07
Retail sales reached $69.9 billion in August, an increase of 0.6% compared with the same month one year earlier. Higher sales were reported in 7 of the 18 commodity classes.
The advance estimate provided by the Monthly Retail Trade Survey suggests that unadjusted total retail sales in September decreased by 2.5%. Because of its preliminary nature, this figure will be revised.
In August, the largest monthly increase in dollar terms came from sales of food and beverages, which rose 5.0% compared with the same month in 2023. In August 2024, sales of eggs and dairy products (+10.3%) led the gains in this commodity class, followed by sales of fresh meat and poultry (+7.6%).
Retail sales of motor vehicles (+2.7%) grew in August. The largest gain within this commodity class was in sales of new motor vehicles (+4.3%), led by sales of new minivans, sport utility vehicles and light trucks (+7.3%). Used motor vehicle sales also posted gains in August, increasing 0.5% compared with the same month in the previous year.
In August, the largest decline in dollar terms came from sales of automotive and household fuels ( 4.8%). With lower prices at the pump, sales of automotive fuels posted a decline of 6.0% compared with the same month in 2023. 
Sales of hardware, tools, and renovation and lawn and garden products ( 5.2%) also saw a decline in August 2024. Sales of lumber and other renovation materials and supplies ( 4.7%) led the decline.
