Article Title:
The Daily — Inter-corporate ownership: Data product, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 06 17
The latest issue of Inter corporate Ownership is now available.
This product is a directory of corporate ownership in Canada. It provides information on each individual corporation that is part of a group of commonly controlled corporations with combined assets exceeding $600 million, or with combined revenue exceeding $200 million. Individual corporations with debt obligations or equity owing to non residents exceeding a net book value of $1 million are covered as well.
The data disseminated in the Inter Corporate Ownership product are not confidential, as they are collected in Schedule 1   Ownership under the authority of the Corporations Returns Act, C 43. 
The 2023 issue of Inter corporate Ownership (Catalogue number61 517 X) is now available online.
