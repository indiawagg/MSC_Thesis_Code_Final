Article Title:
The Daily — Health Regions: Boundaries and Correspondence with Census Geography, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 03 27
The publication Health Regions: Boundaries and Correspondence with Census Geography, September 2023 is now available.
Health regions are defined by the provinces and represent areas or regions of interest to health authorities. 
This publication describes in detail the health region limits as of September 2023 and their correspondence with the 2021 and 2016 census geography. This publication contains correspondence files (linking health regions to census geographic codes), boundary files, and reference maps.
The publication Health Regions: Boundaries and Correspondence with Census Geography (Catalogue number82 402 X) is now available.
