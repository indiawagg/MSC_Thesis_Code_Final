Article Title:
The Daily — Asphalt roofing material, April 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2023 06 05
Production of asphalt shingles totalled 2.4 million metric bundles in April, down 32.6% from March and down 45.1% from April 2022.
Shipments of asphalt shingles totalled 1.7 million metric bundles in April, down 22.0% from the previous month and down 44.3% from April 2022.
