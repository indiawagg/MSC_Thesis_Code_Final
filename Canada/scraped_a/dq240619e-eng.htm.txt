Article Title:
The Daily — Population estimates and demographic dependency ratio: Health regions and peer groups, July 1, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 06 19
Population estimates and demographic dependency ratio by health region and peer group on July 1, 2023, are now available. Updated estimates for previous years are also available.
The estimates released today are based on 2021 Census of Population counts, adjusted for census net undercoverage and incompletely enumerated reserves and settlements, to which are added the population growth estimates for the period from May 11, 2021, to the date of the estimate.
