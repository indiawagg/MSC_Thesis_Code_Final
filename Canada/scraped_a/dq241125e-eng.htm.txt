Article Title:
The Daily — Monthly Survey of Manufacturing: Advance indicator, October 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 25
Statistics Canada is providing an advance estimate of sales in the manufacturing sector for October. Advance results indicate that total manufacturing sales increased 1.3% in October. The largest increases were in the petroleum and coal product as well as transportation equipment subsectors. 
This estimate was calculated based on a weighted response rate of 67.8%. The average final response rate for the survey over the previous 12 months has been 94.0%. As Statistics Canada continues to collect data for the October reference month, this early indicator is subject to a higher revision rate than our regular monthly release.
