Article Title:
The Daily — Foreign direct investment: Data tables
Article Content:
Released: 2023 10 17
To facilitate comparability, annual estimates on foreign direct investment now include complete sets of harmonized geographical groupings across all data tables. These tabular improvements were generated referencing the same data inputs as the official annual release published on April 28, 2023.
