Article Title:
The Daily — Hours worked and labour productivity in the provinces and territories, 1997 to 2022 (revised data)
Article Content:
Released: 2024 02 09
Revised data on provincial and territorial labour productivity and related measures from 1997 to 2022 are now available.
These data revisions reflect the incorporation of the new benchmark values from the provincial and territorial supply and use tables for 2020, as well as revised data from different sources. More specifically, the revisions are consistent with those incorporated in the provincial and territorial economic accounts and the provincial and territorial gross domestic product (GDP) by industry (released November 8, 2023), as well as in the national GDP by industry (released on November 30, 2023).
This release also reflects historical revisions to the Survey of Employment, Payrolls and Hours (released March 30, 2023).
With this release, the year 2017 has been adopted as the reference year instead of 2012 for real GDP data and related measures (including productivity and unit labour costs). The adoption of the new 2017 reference year affects only the data reported in table 36 10 0480 01; it had no impact on the data of the three other tables (36 10 0489 01, 36 10 0675 01 and 36 10 0676 01).
