Article Title:
The Daily — Business and employment dynamics data, 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2024 04 25
Annual 2021 data on business and employment dynamics in the private sector for Canada and every province and the territories, broken down by North American Industry Classification System industry or by firm size class, are now available.
These data are derived from the 2021 Longitudinal Employment Analysis Program files and are added to existing data tables that contain annual estimates going back to 2001.
