Article Title:
The Daily — Waste Management Survey: Waste Disposal, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 07 02
Almost 27 million tonnes of non hazardous waste went to private and public waste disposal facilities in Canada in 2022, an increase of about 2% since 2020.
Disposal of non residential waste amounted to 15.9 million tonnes, representing 60% of all waste disposed, while waste from Canadian households accounted for the remaining 40% (10.7 million tonnes).
