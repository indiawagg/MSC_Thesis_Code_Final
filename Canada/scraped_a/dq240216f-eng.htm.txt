Article Title:
The Daily — Annual Survey of Manufacturing and Logging Industries: Raw materials purchased or used, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 02 16
Data for 2022 and 2021 (revised) on the value of products purchased and used as a raw material by all manufacturing and logging industries are now available upon request for Canada, the provinces and territories.
