Article Title:
The Daily — Oilseed crushing statistics, August 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 09 25
Oilseed processors crushed 850 529 tonnes of canola in August. Oil production totalled 363 165 tonnes, while meal production totalled 494 028 tonnes.
