Article Title:
The Daily — Annual Survey of Manufacturing and Logging Industries: Products produced and sold and Raw materials purchased or used, 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2023 03 06
Data for 2021 and for 2020 (revised) on the value of products produced and sold by all manufacturing and logging industries are now available upon request for Canada and the provinces and territories.
