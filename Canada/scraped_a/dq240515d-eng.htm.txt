Article Title:
The Daily — New entrants to postsecondary education, by Indigenous identity
Article Content:
Released: 2024 05 15
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing data related to new entrants to college and university (entry cohorts of 2015 to 2021) by Indigenous identity. This release includes information on the number of new students in Canada by Indigenous identity, educational qualification, field of study, age group, and gender. Data are available at the national, provincial and territorial levels.
The data used for the calculation of these counts are derived from the Postsecondary Student Information System (PSIS) and the Census of Population.
The data visualization tool titled "Highlights on Indigenous new entrants to postsecondary education" is now available.
