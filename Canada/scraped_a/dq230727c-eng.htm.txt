Article Title:
The Daily — Study: Corporate profitability and inflation, 2017 to 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2023 07 27
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing the study "Corporate profitability and inflation: 2017 to 2022."
This paper looks at how profitability has evolved for key Canadian industries from 2017 to 2022. A specific focus is given to the post lockdown period from the second quarter of 2021 to the second quarter of 2022, during which year over year inflation exceeded the Bank of Canada's inflation control target range ceiling of 3%. In addition to industry wide trends, energy and food sectors are analyzed given their day to day relevance for Canadians.
