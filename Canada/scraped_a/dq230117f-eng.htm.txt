The Daily — Market Basket Measure thresholds, 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2023 01 17
The table Market Basket Measure (MBM) thresholds for the reference family by MBM region, component and base year has been updated to include reference year 2021 thresholds.
The publication "Market Basket Measure poverty thresholds and provisional poverty trends for 2021 and 2022," which is part of the Income Research Paper Series (Catalogue number75F0002M), is now available.
