Article Title:
The Daily — New products: Instruction in the minority official language, 2021 Census of Population
Article Content:
Released: 2022 12 15
Today, Statistics Canada released additional products on instruction in the minority official language from the 2021 Census of Population.
These products complement the article on instruction in the minority official language released in The Daily on November 30, 2022.
Results on instruction in the minority official language are now available through the Census Profile, data tables and thematic maps, which show data for various standard geographic areas.
The infographic "Children eligible for instruction in the minority official language, 2021" is also available.
The concept video "Instruction in the minority official language, 2021 Census of Population" is also available.
