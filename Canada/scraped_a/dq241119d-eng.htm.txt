Article Title:
The Daily — Railway carloadings, September 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 19
In September, the volume of cargo carried by Canadian railways edged down 0.8% from September 2023 to 30.9 million tonnes. Higher shipments of iron ores as well as some agricultural and food products moderated the overall decline. 
Freight traffic volume was just below the five year average of 31.2 tonnes for the month of September. On a year to date basis through September 2024, the volume of goods moved by rail totalled 280.0 million tonnes, slightly higher (+0.8%) than that transported during the same nine months in 2023.
To further explore the latest data and historical trends in an interactive format, please visit the "Monthly Railway Carloadings: Interactive Dashboard."
The small decline in total freight carried in September 2024 reflected lower volumes across all types of rail operations: non intermodal loadings (mainly commodities) and intermodal loadings (mainly containers), as well as freight traffic from connections with American railways. 
In September, non intermodal freight loadings in Canada edged down 0.1% year over year to 24.4 million tonnes. Loadings of potash posted a year over year decrease for a second consecutive month, down 6.9% ( 145 000 tonnes) from September 2023. 
Year over year decreases were also reported for loadings of other oil seeds and nuts, other agricultural products, down 62.1% ( 144 000 tonnes) in September 2024, while wood pulp loadings fell 26.3% ( 120 000 tonnes) over the same period. 
Several commodities registered large increases in September, partly offsetting the above noted declines. Iron ores and concentrates led the increase, with loadings rising by 7.0% (+297 000 tonnes) from September 2023.
Carloadings of certain agricultural and food products also increased in September 2024. Loadings of fresh, chilled, or dried vegetables grew 44.7% (+251 000 tonnes) from September 2023. Similarly, loadings of canola increased for the sixth consecutive month, up 22.0% (+146 000 tonnes) in September from the same month in 2023.
In September 2024, intermodal shipments mainly containers  originating in Canada edged down ( 0.7%) year over year to 2.8 million tonnes, the lowest volume recorded for the month of September in eight years. 
In September 2024, 3.6 million tonnes of rail freight were received from the United States, down 5.0%, or 193 000 tonnes, from September of the previous year.
