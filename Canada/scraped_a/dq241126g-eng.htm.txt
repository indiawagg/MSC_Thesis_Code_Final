Article Title:
The Daily — Wholesale trade: Early indicator, October 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 26
Statistics Canada is providing an advance estimate of sales in the wholesale trade sector for October. The advance results for October indicate that wholesale sales (excluding petroleum, petroleum products, and other hydrocarbons and excluding oilseed and grain) rose 0.5%. The increase partly reflects higher sales in the motor vehicle and motor vehicle parts and accessories subsector.
This estimate was calculated based on a weighted response rate of 62.2%. The average final response rate to the survey over the 12 preceding months was 81.7%. As Statistics Canada continues to collect data for the October reference month, this early indicator is subject to a higher revision rate than the regular monthly release.
