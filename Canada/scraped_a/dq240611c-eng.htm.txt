Article Title:
The Daily — Health region peer groups, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 06 11
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing the document Health Region Peer Groups – Working paper, 2023. This paper presents the classification of the 2023 health regions into peer groups based on similar socioeconomic determinants of health. The purpose of this paper is to define the concept of health region peer groups, to provide an overview of how they are created, and to demonstrate their usefulness.
The publication Health Region Peer Groups – Working paper, 2023 (Catalogue number82 622 X), is now available.
