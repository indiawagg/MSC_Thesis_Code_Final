Article Title:
The Daily — Adult criminal court and youth court, preliminary quarterly data, first quarter of 2024/2025
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 13
Preliminary quarterly data on adult criminal court and youth court for the first quarter of 2024/2025 are now available. 
Data can be found in the product "Integrated Criminal Court Survey: Interactive Dashboard on Preliminary Quarterly Data."
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
