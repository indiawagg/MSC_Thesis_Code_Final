Article Title:
The Daily — Labour market outcomes for college and university graduates
Article Content:
Released: 2024 04 17
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing data on labour market outcomes for college and university graduates for the graduating classes of 2010 to 2020. This release includes information on the median employment income by educational qualification, field of study, gender, age group and status of student in Canada, and is available at the national, provincial and territorial levels. 
The data used for the calculation of graduate outcome indicators are derived from the Postsecondary Student Information System integrated with the T1 Family File and the Administrative Personal Income Masterfile.
The technical reference guide for this release, entitled "Labour market outcomes for college and university graduates, 2010 to 2020" (37200001), is now available.
The data visualization tool, entitled "Labour market outcomes for college and university graduates: Interactive tool," is also available, as part of the series Statistics Canada ― Data Visualization Products (Catalogue number71 607 X).
