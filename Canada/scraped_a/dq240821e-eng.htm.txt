Article Title:
The Daily — Police-reported crime statistics: Data now available in the Canadian Statistical Geospatial Explorer, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 08 21
Updated police reported crime indicators are now available in the Canadian Statistical Geospatial Explorer (CSGE). The CSGE allows users to visually explore data on topics, such as the Crime Severity Index, crime rate, hate crime, cybercrime, organized crime, homicide, and police resources. The data can be viewed at various levels of geography and for multiple years.
New data were released in the "Canadian Statistical Geospatial Explorer Hub" (Catalogue number71 607 X) today.
