Article Title:
The Daily — Prepared Food and Beverage Sales Survey, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 08
Data from the 2023 Prepared Food and Beverage Sales Survey for Ontario are now available upon request.
The survey collected information about the total sales of prepared food and non alcoholic beverages, and the percentage of those sales that were exempt from the provincial portion of the Harmonized Sales Tax.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
