Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian Tobacco and Nicotine Survey (Public Use Microdata File), 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2023 10 11
Data related to the Canadian Tobacco and Nicotine Survey (CTNS) are now available via a Public Use Microdata File. Statistics Canada conducted the fourth cycle of the CTNS from November 2022 to January 2023 in collaboration with Health Canada to obtain information about vaping and smoking habits. These results have suggested that vaping continues to be prevalent among young people in Canada.
