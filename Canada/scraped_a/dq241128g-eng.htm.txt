Article Title:
The Daily — Canada's population estimates: Census families and households, 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 28
Estimates of the number of census families and households for Canada, the provinces and the territories on July 1, for the years 2023 and 2024, are now available. The estimates of the number of census families are broken down by family structure (couple or one parent). The estimates of the number of households are broken down by size. Revised estimates on July 1 for the years 2016 to 2022 are also available.
