Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian new entrants to postsecondary education, by racialized group
Article Content:
Released: 2023 10 26
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing data related to the new entrants to college and university (entry cohorts of 2015 to 2020) by racialized group. This release includes information on the number of new students by racialized group, examining their educational qualification, field of study, gender, and region of study.
The data used for the calculation of these counts are derived from the Postsecondary Student Information System and the Census of Population.
The data visualization tool titled "Highlights on racialized Canadian new entrants to postsecondary education" is now available.
