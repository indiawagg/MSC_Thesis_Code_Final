Article Title:
The Daily — 2026 Census of Population Content Consultation – Reminder
Article Content:
Released: 2023 03 02
We want to hear from you!
To ensure that the census remains responsive to the most pressing and emerging data needs of the country, Statistics Canada is conducting a consultation on content for the 2026 Census of Population. Listening to Canadians is an important step in determining and finalizing content for the 2026 Census. 
Consultation is now underway. For more information on the process or to complete the consultation questionnaire, please visit the 2026 Census of Population Content Consultation webpage.
Your feedback is valuable. Please submit your completed questionnaire by March 31, 2023.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
