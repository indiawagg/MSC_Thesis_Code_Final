Article Title:
The Daily — New historical data tables on interprovincial migration by language
Article Content:
Released: 2023 11 07
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing two new historical data tables on interprovincial migration by mother tongue and by first official language spoken.
A table with information on interprovincial migration by mother tongue for intercensal periods going from 1966 1971 to 2016 2021 and a table on interprovincial migration by first official language spoken for intercensal periods going from 1966 1971 to 2016 2021 are now available.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
