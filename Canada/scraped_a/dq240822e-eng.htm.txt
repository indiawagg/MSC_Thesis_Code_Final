Article Title:
The Daily — Study: Flash estimates of selected services industries, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 08 22
Today, Statistics Canada is publishing a new study entitled "Shifting Economic Conditions Impact Service Industries in 2023." According to this study, service industries are navigating through volatility and adjusting to fundamental economic changes. Most service industries saw slower revenue growth in 2023 compared with 2022. Nonetheless, compared with pre COVID 19 pandemic years, revenue in service industries continued showing significant expansion.
There were notable exceptions, especially in informatics services, where demand remained strong in 2023, primarily attributable to the accelerating digitalization of the economy and outsourcing of information technology needs.
The study "Shifting Economic Conditions Impact Service Industries in 2023," which is part of Analysis in Brief (Catalogue number11 621 M), is now available.
