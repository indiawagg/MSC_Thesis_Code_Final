Article Title:
The Daily — Sawmills, August 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 04
Lumber production increased 2.5% from July to 3 772.6 thousand cubic metres in August. Production was down 9.9% from August 2023.
Sawmills shipped 3 835.1 thousand cubic metres of lumber in August, down 0.8% from July and down 2.1% from August 2023. 
Table 16 10 0018 01, "Canadian lumber exports by mode of transportation," is available. This table contains annual data by province and territory from 1999 to 2023. It replaces Table 9 from the monthly publication Sawmills (Catalogue number35 003 X), which was discontinued in October 2017.
