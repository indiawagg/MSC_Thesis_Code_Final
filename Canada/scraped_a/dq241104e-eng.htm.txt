Article Title:
The Daily — Ontario First Nations Point-of-Sale Exemption Survey, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 04
Data from the 2023 Ontario First Nations Point of Sale Exemption Survey are now available upon request. 
This survey collects information on First Nations point of sale exemptions provided to Indian status card holders at the time they purchased selected goods and services off reserve. The exemptions correspond to the 8% provincial portion of the Harmonized Sales Tax in Ontario. 
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
