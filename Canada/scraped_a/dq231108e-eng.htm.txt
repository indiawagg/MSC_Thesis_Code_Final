The Daily — Tables: Premature and potentially avoidable mortality in Canada, provinces and territories, 2018 to 2021

Released: 2023 11 08
New statistics on premature and potentially avoidable mortality are now available. Today, Statistics Canada is releasing updated avoidable mortality counts and rates in table 13 10 0744 01 for 2018 to 2021 and in table 13 10 0753 01 for the 2016 2018 to the 2019 2021 periods.
