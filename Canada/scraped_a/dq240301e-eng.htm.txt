Article Title:
The Daily — Housing in rural and small town Canada, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 03 01
The Rural Canada Housing Profiles (RCHP) data are now available for the 2022 reference year. 
The RCHP database provides users with comprehensive information on housing in rural and small town areas of Canada, by provinces, territories, and municipalities (census subdivisions).
The database Rural Canada Housing Profiles is now available. 
The publication Rural Canada Housing Profiles: metadata and user guide is available. 
The product Rural Canada Housing Profiles: Interactive Dashboard, which is part of Statistics Canada Data Visualization Products (Catalogue number71 607 X), is now available.
For more information, or to enquire about the concepts, methods or data quality of this release, contact us (toll free 1 800 263 1136; 514 283 8300; infostats@statcan.gc.ca) or Media Relations (statcan.mediahotline ligneinfomedias.statcan@statcan.gc.ca).
