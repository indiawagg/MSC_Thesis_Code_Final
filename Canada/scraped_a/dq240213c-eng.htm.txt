Article Title:
The Daily — Chicken production, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 02 13
Chicken production totalled 1.4 million tonnes in 2023, up 3.4% from the previous year. More than 60% of the chicken produced in Canada came from Ontario (477 451 tonnes) and Quebec (369 422 tonnes).
