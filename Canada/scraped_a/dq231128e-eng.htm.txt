Article Title:
The Daily — Canadian Health Measures Survey: Vitamin D status of Canadians, 2007 to 2019
Article Content:
Released: 2023 11 28
The Canadian Health Measures Survey is releasing today a new table presenting the vitamin D status of Canadians aged 3 to 79. This table provides the prevalence of vitamin D deficiency, inadequacy, and sufficiency, by age group and sex for the period from 2007 to 2019.
