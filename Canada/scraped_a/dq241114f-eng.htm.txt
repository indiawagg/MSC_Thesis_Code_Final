Article Title:
The Daily — National and provincial input-output multipliers, 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 14
The national and provincial input output multipliers for 2021 are now available. 
The national and interprovincial input output model simulations for 2021 are also available.
The service Input Output Model Simulations (National Model) (Catalogue number36230001) is now based on the 2021 supply and use tables. 
The service Input Output Model Simulations (Interprovincial Model) (Catalogue number36230002) is now based on the 2021 supply and use tables.
