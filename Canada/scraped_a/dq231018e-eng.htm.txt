Article Title:
The Daily — Public Sector Survey on Cyber Security and Cybercrime, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2023 10 18
Data related to the Public Sector Survey on Cyber Security and Cybercrime are now available. Data from this pilot study provide insights on the impact of cybercrime on the Canadian public service and crown corporations, including the investment in cyber security measures, cyber security training, the volume of cyber security incidents and the costs associated with responding to these incidents.
These data will be made available in the Research Data Centres.
