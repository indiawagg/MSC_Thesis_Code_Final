Article Title:
The Daily — Aquaculture, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 26
In 2023, sales of Canadian aquaculture products and services decreased 4.7% compared with 2022 to $1.3 billion, while product expenses declined 3.0%. Aquaculture sales in British Columbia were down 35.9%, while sales in the Atlantic provinces increased 68.8%. 
Nationally, farmed finfish production fell to 107 144 tonnes in 2023, a 15.7% decline from 2022. While the average farmed finfish price rose 9.7% to $10.52 per kilogram over the same period, the total farmed finfish value decreased 7.5% to $1.1 billion. 
Canadian farmed shellfish production also declined in 2023, down 4.5% to 38 699 tonnes. Despite the decrease in production, farmed shellfish value edged up to $126.5 million (+1.0%), as the average farmed shellfish price increased to $3.27 per kilogram (+5.7%). 
Farmed finfish production in British Columbia declined 41.4% in 2023, compared with the previous year. Consequently, British Columbia represented less than half (47.9%) of national finfish production for the first time since reporting began in 1991. The average price for farmed finfish in British Columbia was $10.18 per kilogram in 2023, a 0.7% increase from 2022.
In 2023, farmed finfish production in the Atlantic provinces increased 52.4% year over year, led by New Brunswick (+137.5%) and Newfoundland and Labrador (+90.1%), as finfish production recovered from environmental and biological issues. The value of farmed finfish in the Atlantic provinces reached a record high in 2023, as the average price rose 25.8%.
In 2023, export quantities for farmed Atlantic salmon including fillets decreased to 64 469 tonnes ( 18.0% compared with 2022). Over the same period, export prices climbed 1.5% to $12.57 per kilogram, resulting in a 16.8% decline in sales to $810.6 million. The United States accounted for 98.6% of total exports of Canadian farmed Atlantic salmon by quantity in 2023. 
Production of shellfish in Prince Edward Island Canada's primary producer of farmed shellfish, accounting for more than half (52.5%) of the national total decreased to 20 684 tonnes ( 4.8%) in 2023, compared with 2022. Farmed shellfish sales accounted for 9.7% of total sales of Canadian aquaculture products and services in 2023. 
Mussel production in Prince Edward Island, the largest producer of mussels in Canada, declined 10.9% year over year, totalling 15 543 tonnes in 2023. British Columbia, the largest producer of oysters, produced 7 371 tonnes of oysters in 2023, 11.8% less than in 2022. 
Export quantities of mussels decreased 10.3% to 11 296 tonnes in 2023, compared with 2022. Despite this decrease, the price of exported mussels increased 11.8%, yielding a 0.3% increase in total export value to $49.3 million.
