Article Title:
The Daily — Canada's population estimates, second quarter 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 09 25
Population estimates for Canada, the provinces, and territories on July 1, 2024, are now available. These new data allow for an analysis of population growth during the second quarter of 2024. Updated estimates from the third quarter of 2021 to the first quarter of 2024 are also available. Demographic estimates up to and including the second quarter of 2021 are final. 
The product "Quarterly demographic estimates, provinces and territories: Interactive dashboard" (Catalogue number71 607 X) is also available. 
The product "Canada's population clock (real time model)" (Catalogue number71 607 X) is also available.
The Population and demography statistics and Older adults and population aging statistics portals are also available.
