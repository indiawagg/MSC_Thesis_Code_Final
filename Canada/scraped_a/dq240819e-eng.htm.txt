Article Title:
The Daily — Industrial chemicals and synthetic resins, 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2024 08 19
In 2023, the highest production levels for industrial chemicals were recorded for ethylene (5.1 million tonnes, up 5.4% from 2022), anhydrous ammonia (4.8 million tonnes, up 7.4%) and urea (4.1 million tonnes, up 16.7%).
The production of polyethylene, a synthetic resin, reached 3.8 million tonnes, an increase of 5.8% from 2022.
