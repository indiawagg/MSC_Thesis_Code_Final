Article Title:
The Daily — Dental insurance coverage, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2024 02 07
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing data on the number of Canadians who have insurance that covers dental care as well as the number who have had a recent visit with a dental professional. The data are available in two tables using results from the 2022 cycle of the Canadian Community Health Survey.
