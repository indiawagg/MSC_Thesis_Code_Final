Article Title:
The Daily — Capital expenditures on environmental protection by businesses, 2019 to 2021
Article Content:
Released: 2024 10 16
Data on capital expenditures on environmental protection for the years 2019, 2020 and 2021 are now available. The data present estimates on industry groups not covered by the Environmental Protection Expenditures Survey, which provides a measure of the costs incurred by Canadian industries to protect the environment.
