Article Title:
The Daily — Number and salaries of full-time teaching staff at Canadian universities (partial), 2023/2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 04 29
Today, Statistics Canada is releasing the first results from the Full time University and College Academic Staff System  survey for the 2023/2024 academic year.
This partial release includes data from 87 of the 111 public degree granting institutions that had finalized data available by the beginning of April 2024. A final release for the 2023/2024 academic year, with data for all institutions, will be available in the fall of 2024. It will provide a detailed portrait of full time academic staff in Canadian universities, including gender, age, academic rank, salary and administrative stipends, administrative responsibilities, and principal subject taught.
