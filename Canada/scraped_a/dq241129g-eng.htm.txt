Article Title:
The Daily — Poultry and egg statistics, November 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 29
Data on stocks of frozen eggs and poultry meats, as well as edible dried egg products, are now available for November 2024. Data on the placements of hatchery chicks and turkey poults are available for October 2024, and data on egg production are available for September 2024.
Data on frozen eggs and poultry meats include chicken, turkey and duck stocks, as well as stocks of egg whites, egg yolks and dried eggs. Data on egg production include production, disposition, home consumption and egg prices.
