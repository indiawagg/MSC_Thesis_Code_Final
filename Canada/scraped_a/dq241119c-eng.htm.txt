Article Title:
The Daily — Monthly credit aggregates, September 2024
Article Content:
Released: 2024 11 19
In September, the total credit liabilities of households rose 0.4% (+$10.5 billion) to reach $2,993.1 billion, following a 0.3% increase in August. Real estate secured debt, composed of both mortgage debt and home equity lines of credit, grew 0.4% (+$8.6 billion) in September. Household mortgage debt increased 0.4% (+$8.0 billion), an acceleration compared with August (+0.3%). In September, the policy interest rate decreased 25 basis points to 4.25%.
Non mortgage loan debt increased 0.3% (+$2.5 billion) in September, following a 0.1% rise in August. In September, outstanding balances on home equity lines of credit increased 0.4% (+$0.7 billion) and credit card debt with chartered banks rose 0.7% (+$0.8 billion). The remaining non mortgage loan debt categories increased $1.0 billion following a $0.1 billion decrease in August.
Total credit liabilities of private non financial corporations edged up 0.2% (+$4.6 billion) to reach $2,124.5 billion in September, a faster rise than in August (+0.1%). Non mortgage loans with chartered banks edged down 0.2% ( $1.4 billion) in September, a smaller decline than in August ( 0.4%;  $2.3 billion). The already reduced balance of bankers' acceptances remaining after the cessation of the Canadian Dollar Offered Rate's publication in June 2024 dropped further in September to $0.1 billion. Non mortgage loans from government lenders edged down 0.2% ( $0.1 billion), while debt securities rose 0.5% (+$3.8 billion).
Overall borrowing by private non financial corporations, composed of the change in the level of seasonally unadjusted outstanding loans and the net transactions in debt securities, was $3.2 billion in September.
