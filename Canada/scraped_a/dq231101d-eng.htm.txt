Article Title:
The Daily — Financial market statistics, September 2023
Article Content:
Released: 2023 11 01
Data on financial markets are now available for September. These data tables will no longer be produced by Statistics Canada since the data are freely available from other public sources. The Bank of Canada data can be found on the page Bank of Canada assets and liabilities: Weekly (formerly B2), while the stock market statistics are available on the page Canadian Stock Market Summary Today of the TMX website.
The Economic accounts statistics portal, accessible from the Subjects module of the Statistics Canada website, features an up to date portrait of national and provincial economies and their structure.
The Latest Developments in the Canadian Economic Accounts (Catalogue number13 605 X) is available.
The User Guide: Canadian System of Macroeconomic Accounts (Catalogue number13 606 G) is also available.
The Methodological Guide: Canadian System of Macroeconomic Accounts (Catalogue number13 607 X) is also available.
