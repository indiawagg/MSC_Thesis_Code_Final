Article Title:
The Daily — National Travel Survey: Public use microdata file, 2022
Article Content:
Released: 2023 11 24
The National Travel Survey (NTS) public use microdata file (PUMF) for 2022 is now available.
This PUMF contains data for a wide variety of variables collected from the 2022 NTS that cover the characteristics of Canadian residents travelling within Canada and abroad: travel expenditures, length of stay, type of transportation, purpose of trip, accommodation used, places visited, and expenditures by category.
The product National Travel Survey microdata file, 2022 (Catalogue number24250001), is now available.
