Date:
2020-12-21
Article Title:
 Sub-annual motor vehicle use estimates, July 2019 to June 2020

Article Content:
This article provides estimates on Australian motor vehicle use and freight movements over the 2019/20 year for the following four month collection periods: 
Period 1 – 1 July 2019 to 31 October 2019Period 2 – 1 November 2019 to 29 February 2020 (bushfires and severe weather events)Period 3 – 1 March 2020 to 30 June 2020 (COVID 19)
These estimates have been produced to inform any impacts on vehicle use during the 2019/20 summer bushfires and the first four months of the COVID 19 pandemic. Collection period 2 aligns with natural disasters experienced over 2019/20 and period 3 aligns with the first four months of the COVID 19 pandemic.
Included in this publication for reference and comparison are sub annual estimates for the Survey of Motor Vehicle Use, 2018.  These estimates are available as a data download.
In Period 1: 
  New South Wales registered vehicles travelled the most with 28,728 million kilometres, this accounted for 32.0% of all kilometres travelled 
  Victoria contributed the second largest number of kilometres, with 23,432 million kilometres (26.1%), followed by Queensland with 18,169 million kilometres (20.2%)
  The Northern Territory contributed the least, travelling 757 million kilometres (0.8%)
In Period 1 motor vehicles travelled a total of 89,767 million kilometres. 
  Passenger vehicles contributed the most and travelled 62,063 million kilometres (69.1%)
  Light commercial vehicles travelled 19,289 million kilometres (21.5%)
  Rigid trucks travelled 3,912 million kilometres (4.4%)
  Articulated trucks travelled 2,913 million kilometres (3.2%)
  Motor cycles, non freight carrying trucks and buses travelled 1,590 million kilometres (1.8%)
  Total fuel consumed by all vehicles was 12,219 megalitres
Compared to Period 1:
  New South Wales registered vehicles drove 21,386 million kilometres, down 7,342 million kilometres (25.6%)
  New South Wales vehicles used less fuel consuming 3,001 megalitres, down 705 megalitres (19.0%)
  Western Australian vehicles were the only vehicles to record an increase, travelling 9,956 million kilometres, up 7.1%
Compared to Period 1: 
  Kilometres travelled by all vehicles was 82,028 million kilometres, down 8.6%
  Passenger vehicles accounted for the largest decrease in kilometres, travelling 57,113 million kilometres, down 4,950 million kilometres (8.0%)
  Light commercial vehicles had the second largest decrease, travelling 17,333 million kilometres, down 1,956 million kilometres (10.1%)
  Fuel consumed by all vehicles was 11,105 megalitres, down 9.1%
Compared to Period 2: 
  All State and Territories except Tasmania experienced a decrease in kilometres travelled in period 3
  Motor vehicle use in Tasmania increased by 5.5% to 1,816 million kilometres
  Victorian registered vehicles had the largest decrease, travelling 16,728 million kilometres, down 6,570 million kilometres (28.2%)
  Queensland registered vehicles had the second largest decrease, travelling 14,319 million kilometres, down 3,839 million kilometres (21.1%)
Compared to Period 2: 
  Kilometres travelled by all vehicles was 66,334 million kilometres, down 19.1%
  Passenger vehicles accounted for the largest decrease, travelling 43,625 million kilometres, down 13,488 million kilometres (23.6%)
  Light commercials had the second largest decrease, travelling 15,423 million kilometres, down 1,910 million kilometres (11.0%)
  Articulated trucks had the largest increase with 2,755 million kilometres travelled, up 245 million kilometres (9.8%) 
  Fuel consumed by all vehicles was 9,657 megalitres, down 13.0%, reflecting the reduction in distance travelled
  Freight movement, measured in tonne kilometres, reduced slightly during the natural disasters and COVID 19 pandemic experienced during periods 2 and 3. 
  Freight vehicles in period 1 travelled 82,027 million tonne kilometres. This decreased to 68,582 million tonne kilometres in period 2, down 16.4%. From period 2 to period 3 there was an increase of 6.6% with 73,090 tonne kilometres.
  Articulated trucks travelled 62,931 million tonne kilometres in period 1. This decreased to 53,479 tonne kilometres in period 2, down 15.0%. From period 2 to period 3 there was an increase of 6.0% with 56,673 tonne kilometres travelled.
  Rigid trucks and light commercial vehicles showed decreases in tonne kilometres travelled from period 1 to 2. Rigid trucks decreased by 20.2% and light commercial vehicles decreased by 24.1%.  Both showed increases from period 2 to period 3, with rigid trucks travelling 13,725 million tonne kilometres in period 3 (up 10.4%) and light commercial vehicles travelling 2,692 million tonne kilometres in period 3 (up 0.9%).
